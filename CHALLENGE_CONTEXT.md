## 2. OFFICIAL PROBLEM CONTEXT

Governments often struggle to consolidate citizen feedback and align it with national infrastructure priorities.

Development requests from citizens are fragmented across channels, regions, languages, and administrative boundaries.

As a result, policymakers may lack a unified intelligence layer that allows them to understand:

- what citizens are collectively asking for
- where those needs are concentrated
- what infrastructure/service gaps exist
- what demographic and development context surrounds those needs
- what public investments or interventions already exist
- which development needs should receive greater attention
- how large-scale digital public initiatives can be evaluated against real public need

The challenge therefore requires a solution that can transform fragmented citizen demand and public information into useful development-planning intelligence.

---

## 3. OFFICIAL CHALLENGE

Build a scalable, multilingual AI platform — designed as a Digital Public Good — that aggregates citizen development requests via:

- voice
- text
- messaging

across diverse linguistic regions.

The system should analyze citizen feedback together with relevant public information such as:

- demographic data
- infrastructure indicators
- public investment plans

The goal is to:

- surface demand hotspots
- identify areas of significant unmet development need
- support evidence-based prioritization
- recommend high-priority development projects
- provide actionable intelligence to national policymakers

The solution should be designed to scale across states, communities, and eventually BRICS nations rather than being limited to a single locality.

---

## 4. CORE EXPECTED CAPABILITIES

The solution should demonstrate an end-to-end flow connecting citizen demand with public-development intelligence.

Core capabilities expected from the challenge include:

### Citizen Input

The system should support development-related citizen requests through appropriate channels such as:

- text
- voice
- messaging

The experience should be multilingual / language-inclusive where applicable.

### AI Understanding

AI should be capable of understanding fragmented citizen expressions and extracting meaningful development needs from them.

Different ways of expressing similar problems should be capable of being interpreted as a related underlying development need.

### Public Data Integration

The system should be capable of combining citizen information with appropriate public datasets, such as:

- demographics
- infrastructure/service indicators
- public development information
- investment or planning information
- other relevant public/open datasets

### Geographic Intelligence

The system should identify where development needs are concentrated and support geographic analysis of demand and public infrastructure/service conditions.

### Demand Hotspots

The platform should identify geographic areas where meaningful citizen demand is concentrated.

### Development Gap Identification

The solution should help identify where citizen demand and measured public/infrastructure conditions suggest an unmet development need.

### Prioritization

The system should support identification of higher-priority development needs based on evidence and relevant factors.

### Policymaker Decision Support

The final output should be useful to policymakers and should support actionable development planning rather than merely displaying raw complaints.

### Scalability

The system should be designed so that its approach can extend beyond one locality and beyond one narrow implementation.

### Digital Public Good Orientation

The solution should be designed with public usefulness, scalability, transparency, extensibility, and real-world applicability in mind.

## 8. EVALUATION PARAMETERS

Submissions are evaluated across five major criteria.

### Problem-Solution Fit — 20%

Does the solution directly and specifically address the challenge?

The judges should be able to understand the relationship between the problem and the proposed solution.

### AI / Technical Execution — 25%

How well is Google AI / AI technically and meaningfully integrated into the working prototype?

The prototype should demonstrate real technical execution rather than superficial AI usage.

### Depth & Reach Across India — 20%

Can the solution realistically scale from one city/community/locality to broader states or communities across India?

The architecture and approach should support wider geographic applicability.

### Deployability & Scalability — 20%

Could the prototype realistically be piloted within a ministry, public institution, or broader government/public infrastructure environment?

The system should have a credible path toward scaling.

### Impact Potential — 15%

What is the potential benefit of the solution?

This includes considerations such as:

- how many people could benefit
- how many communities/states could benefit
- the significance of the development problem
- potential public value

Total:
100%
