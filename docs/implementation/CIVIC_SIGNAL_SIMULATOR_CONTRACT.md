# Civic Signal Simulator Contract

## 1. Purpose
The Civic Signal Simulator is a controlled scenario generator designed strictly for demonstration and hackathon validation purposes. Because the MVP dataset lacks real citizen-demand signals, the simulator provides deterministic, realistic demand signals to prove the convergence pipeline works.

**Crucially, simulated signals are NOT empirical evidence.** They represent hypotheticals.

## 2. Synthetic Provenance
Every signal generated by the simulator is indelibly marked as synthetic.
- `is_synthetic` = `True`
- `source` = `"SIMULATOR"`
- `provenance` = `"SYNTHETIC_DEMO_SIGNAL"`
- `synthetic_scenario_id` = The explicit scenario used (e.g., `WATER_CONVERGENCE`).

This structure guarantees that no UI or audit ledger will ever accidentally classify these records as real citizen submissions.

## 3. Scenario Catalog
The simulator supports a bounded catalog of scenarios:
- **`WATER_CONVERGENCE`**: Generates water-supply complaints targeting a real village that has a known infrastructure gap (JJM `reported_household_gap > 0`).
- **`WATER_NO_PUBLIC_GAP`**: Generates water-supply complaints targeting a real village that claims to have complete coverage (JJM `reported_household_gap == 0`), proving the system's ability to detect public/citizen divergence.
- **`NON_WATER_DEMAND`**: Generates road or health complaints to demonstrate semantic filtering and unsupported-sector rejection.
- **`UNRESOLVED_GEOGRAPHY`**: Generates complaints lacking location data to validate the unresolved geographic pathway.

## 4. Real vs. Synthetic Context Distinction
When a scenario targets a real village, it pulls the `lgd_village_code` from the real normalized analytics dataset to establish the convergence context.
However, **the synthetic citizen text never magically knows or quotes the JJM statistics**. The text simulates a citizen's lived experience only.

## 5. Persistence Boundary
Synthetic signals are **never** written to `data/raw/` or `data/normalized/signals.jsonl`. They are written exclusively to `data/local_development/synthetic_citizen_signals.jsonl`.
